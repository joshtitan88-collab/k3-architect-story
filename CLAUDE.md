## Behavioral rules
- Dark, operator-grade, quiet luxury. Not SaaS-gradient slop.
- One idea per viewport. Generous whitespace.
- No fake testimonials, no invented clients besides the existing Acme HVAC demo.
- No health/finance/legal/political claims.
- Unlock-style CTA. Privacy is architecture, not a badge farm.
- Copy is specific: hours, ROI table, 90-day pilot — from the source site.
- Video/images contain no readable text or logos.
